Gum Arabic (Acacia Gum) — Premium Natural Hydrocolloid

Gum Arabic (Acacia Gum) is the dried exudate from Acacia senegal and Acacia seyal. It is a highly water-soluble polysaccharide used globally as a stabilizer, emulsifier, microencapsulation agent, and natural fiber supplement. Dharma Hydrocolloid supplies food-grade and pharma-grade Gum Arabic with traceability and certification for export markets.
