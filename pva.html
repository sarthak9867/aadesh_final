Overview & Applications

Polyvinyl Alcohol (PVA) is produced by hydrolysis of polyvinyl acetate and is prized for film-forming, adhesive, and emulsifying properties. Our portfolio includes multiple commercial grades (BP-20, BP-24, GH-17R, PVA 173, PVA 2488, PVA 2088) to suit adhesives, textile sizing, water-soluble films, paper coating, pharmaceuticals and industrial uses. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Available Grades (short)

BP-20
Partially hydrolyzed (≈87–89%). Good solubility and film formation. Applications: adhesives, sizing, films.
BP-24
Similar to BP-20 with balanced film/adhesion properties — excellent for coatings and adhesives.
GH-17R
Moderate viscosity and solubility; used in coatings, adhesives and water-soluble packaging films.
PVA 173
Moderate viscosity grade used widely in adhesives, films and pharmaceuticals.
PVA 2488
Higher molecular weight/viscosity for demanding adhesives, coatings and protective films.
PVA 2088
Balanced viscosity and solubility for textile sizing, adhesives and paper applications.

Key Properties (typical)

  • Water-soluble; insoluble in common organic solvents.
  • Film-forming, adhesive, binder and emulsifier properties.
  • Degree of hydrolysis typically 87–89% for partially hydrolyzed grades (varies by grade).
  • Molecular weight & viscosity vary by grade — choose grade based on required viscosity and film strength.

Applications (summary)

Adhesives: paper adhesives, woodworking, cold-water adhesives and remoistenable glues.

Textile: warp sizing, improved yarn strength and reduced breakage during weaving.

Films: water-soluble films for detergent pods, single-use packaging, and agricultural formulations.

Paper & Coatings: surface sizing, pigment binder and improved printability.

Pharma & Medical: tablet binders, coatings, hydrogel dressings and controlled-release systems.

Building & Construction: additives for mortar/cement to improve adhesion and water retention (selected grades).

Safety & Storage

PVA is generally low-hazard in handling at ambient conditions. Avoid dust inhalation for powdered forms. Store dry, in cool conditions, in sealed packaging to avoid moisture pickup and caking. Refer to SDS for complete safety and transport instructions.
