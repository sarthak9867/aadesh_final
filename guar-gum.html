Product Details & Grades

Guar gum is a high-molecular-weight galactomannan from guar seed endosperm (Cyamopsis tetragonoloba). It provides powerful thickening and stabilizing ability (up to ~8× starch) and excellent cold-water solubility.

Applications

  • Food & Beverage: thickener, stabilizer, ice-cream stabilizer, gluten-free baking, sauces, dairy & beverages.
  • Pharmaceutical: tablet binder, sustained-release matrix, suspending agent.

  • Industrial: textile sizing, paper coating, mining flocculant, construction additives.

FAQ

Q: Can guar gum replace starch?

A: Yes — guar gum gives much higher thickening power and is often used where clean-label and cold-water solubility are desired.

Q: What is the shelf life?

A: 24 months when stored dry (<25°C), RH <65%.
