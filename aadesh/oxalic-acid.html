Applications & Uses

Textiles: bleaching cotton, linen and silk; removes pigments and iron stains; improves dye uniformity.

Metal Treatment: removes rust and forms water-soluble ferrioxalate complexes for easy dissolution.

Wood & Stone Restoration: bleaches and brightens wood, marble and concrete surfaces.

Water Treatment: chelating agent for heavy metals; assists in pH control and surface prep.

Pulp & Paper: used in bleaching to remove lignin and impurities for high-brightness products.

Semiconductor: used in copper planarization and surface treatments for electronics manufacturing.

Advantages & Benefits

  • Multi-purpose: cleaning, bleaching and rust removal in one chemical.
  • High efficacy and cost-effective performance in industrial processes.
  • Favorable handling and disposal profile when used with appropriate controls.
Safety & Handling

Store in a dry, cool place away from oxidizing agents. Use appropriate PPE (gloves, eye protection, protective clothing). Oxalic acid can be corrosive at high concentrations — follow SDS guidance and local regulations for disposal.
