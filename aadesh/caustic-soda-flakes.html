Industrial Applications & Uses

Soap & Detergent Manufacturing

Caustic soda is the primary reagent in saponification, the chemical process that converts fats and oils into soap. It is essential for producing solid bar soaps and liquid detergents with consistent quality and strong cleaning properties.

Pulp & Paper Industry

Used to break down wood into pulp by dissolving lignin and resins. It is also vital in paper recycling and de-inking processes, allowing fibers to be reused efficiently.

Textile Processing

  • Mercerizing: Improves cotton strength, luster and dye affinity
  • Scouring: Removes natural waxes and impurities
  • Dyeing: Supports dye fixation in nylon and polyester

Water Treatment

Widely used to regulate pH levels, neutralize acidic water, and remove heavy metals in municipal and industrial water treatment plants.

Chemical Manufacturing

Sodium hydroxide is a key reactant in producing solvents, plastics, fabrics, dyes, and numerous chemical intermediates.

Other Key Applications

  • Alumina Production: Refining bauxite ore into alumina
  • Industrial Cleaning: Heavy-duty degreasing and cleaning
  • Petroleum Industry: pH control and contaminant removal
  • Food Processing: Chemical peeling, pretzel browning, mold inhibition
  • Pharmaceuticals: Used in aspirin and cholesterol drug manufacturing

Safety & Handling

Caustic Soda is highly corrosive and can cause severe burns.

• Always use appropriate PPE including gloves and eye protection.
• Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area in tightly sealed containers.
• Prevent exposure to moisture and carbon dioxide.
• Refer to the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for complete handling and disposal instructions.
