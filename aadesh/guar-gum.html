What is Guar Gum?

Guar Gum is a high-molecular-weight polysaccharide belonging to the galactomannan family. It hydrates rapidly in cold water to form highly viscous solutions and provides exceptional thickening, stabilizing, and binding properties.

Key characteristics of our Guar Gum:

  • Cold-water soluble with rapid hydration
  • Viscosity up to 8 times higher than starch
  • Excellent stability over a wide pH range
  • Synergistic performance with xanthan gum and locust bean gum
  • Neutral taste and odor
