What is Gum Olibanum?

Gum Olibanum, or Frankincense, is an oleo-gum resin obtained from the bark of Boswellia serrata and related species. It comprises resin (60–70%), gum (20–30%), and essential oil (5–10%), offering unique aromatic, anti-inflammatory, and therapeutic properties.

Our Gum Olibanum is distinguished by:

  • High resin content (≥65%) for superior aroma and therapeutic efficacy
  • Purity free from bark and foreign matter
  • Rich in boswellic acids (≥20%) for anti-inflammatory benefits
  • Aromatic essential oil (yield 5–8%) with rich incense fragrance
  • Natural granule and tear shapes for varied applications
