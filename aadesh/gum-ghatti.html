Gum Ghatti (Dhavda Gum) – Nature’s Multi-Tasking Hydrocolloid

Gum Ghatti is a plant-derived hydrocolloid obtained from the Anogeissus latifolia tree, known locally as the Dhawda tree. It stands out as a naturally emulsifying, stabilizing, and thickening agent, surpassing traditional gums like Gum Arabic across food, pharmaceutical, oilfield, and industrial applications.

Sustainable Origins

  • Botanical Name: Anogeissus latifolia
  • Common Name: Dhavda Tree
  • Harvest Location: Indian dry deciduous forests
  • Collection Method: Naturally exuded gum, hand-harvested by forest communities
  • Appearance: Pale yellow to amber crystals (varies with age & exposure)

Functional & Chemical Highlights

  • Water Dispersibility: 80–90% (hot or cold water)
  • pH Stability: Naturally buffers around pH 4.8
  • Polymer Composition: L-arabinose, D-galactose, D-mannose, D-xylose & glucuronic acid

Functional Traits

  • ✔ Superior emulsification
  • ✔ High viscosity
  • ✔ Film formation
  • ✔ Long-term stability
