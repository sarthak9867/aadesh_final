Gum Ghatti (Dhavda Gum)

Gum Ghatti is a plant-derived hydrocolloid obtained from the Anogeissus latifolia (Dhawda) tree. It is valued for superior emulsification, high viscosity, film-forming ability and long-term stability. Dharma Hydrocolloid supplies precision-processed Gum Ghatti suitable for food, pharmaceutical, oilfield and various industrial applications.

Available Grades

  • Gum Ghatti Nova 1 — Crude & Powder
  • Gum Ghatti Nova 2 — Crude & Powder
  • Gum Ghatti Nova 3 — Crude & Powder
  • Gum Ghatti Super Nova — Spray-dried powder, high-purity for food & pharma

Functional & Chemical Highlights

  • Water Dispersibility: 80–90% (hot or cold water)
  • pH Stability: naturally buffers near pH 4.8
  • Polymer Composition: L-arabinose, D-galactose, D-mannose, D-xylose & glucuronic acid
  • Key functions: superior emulsification, high viscosity, film formation and long-term stability

Key Applications

Food & Beverage: emulsions (turmeric/lycopene), syrup clarity, natural coatings.

Pharmaceuticals: oil-based vitamin stabilization, controlled release carriers.

Oil & Gas: viscosifier in drilling fluids, acidizing applications.

Industrial: dust-binding emulsions, water-repellent coatings, microencapsulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What makes Dharma’s Gum Ghatti different?

A: We offer customized grades and blends tailored to your processing conditions and end-use.

Q2: Which form should I choose — crude, powder, or spray-dried?

A: Crude for industrial processing; Powder for ease-of-use; Spray-dried for high-precision food/pharma use.

Q3: Can Gum Ghatti replace Gum Arabic?

A: Yes — in many emulsions and stabilizer roles Gum Ghatti outperforms Gum Arabic and is often a cost-effective replacement.
