Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What’s the difference between Sodium Propionate and Calcium Propionate?
A: Sodium Propionate is more soluble and often preferred in non-yeasted products (cakes, tortillas), while Calcium Propionate is preferred where minimal yeast inhibition and calcium fortification are desired.

Q: Is Sodium Propionate safe to consume?
A: Yes — it’s widely recognized as safe by regulatory authorities worldwide and metabolized like other short-chain fatty acids. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
