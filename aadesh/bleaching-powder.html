Product Overview

Bleaching Powder (Calcium Hypochlorite, Ca(OCl)2) is a stable, free-flowing powder with strong oxidizing and disinfecting properties. It produces active chlorine when dissolved and is extensively used in water disinfection, industrial bleaching, and sanitation.

Key Properties & Specifications

Active IngredientCalcium Hypochlorite [Ca(OCl)2]
FormStable, free-flowing white to pale yellow powder
FunctionDisinfectant, bleaching agent, oxidizer
SolubilityDissolves in water to produce sanitizing solution
CAS No7778-54-3

Major Applications

  • Water Treatment & Sanitation: drinking water disinfection, municipal water, wastewater, swimming pools.
  • Textile Industry: bleaching cotton & linen for uniform dyeing and whiteness.
  • Pulp & Paper: whitening wood pulp by removing lignin.
  • Chemical Manufacturing: oxidizing agent and intermediate (e.g., chloroform production).
  • Household & Institutional: surface disinfectant, stain removal, mold & mildew control.
  • Agriculture & Food Processing: equipment disinfection and irrigation water treatment.
Safety & Handling: Strong oxidizer — corrosive. Use appropriate PPE (gloves, goggles, mask). Store in cool, dry, ventilated area away from acids, organics and moisture. Refer to SDS for complete instructions.

Advantages

  • Cost-effective disinfection with broad-spectrum efficacy
  • Stable and easy to transport compared to liquid chlorine
  • Trusted, proven performance across industries

FAQ

Q: Can bleaching powder be used for drinking water?

A: Yes — when dosed correctly. Follow local guidelines and SDS for appropriate concentrations.

Q: How should it be stored?

A: In a cool, dry place away from acids and organic materials to prevent chlorine gas release.
