What is Gum Karaya?

Gum Karaya, also known as Sterculia gum, is a natural exudate obtained from Sterculia urens trees. Rich in acidic polysaccharides, it exhibits high viscosity, excellent swelling, and superior adhesive properties, making it ideal for specialized applications.

Our Gum Karaya features:

  • High molecular weight acidic polysaccharide (~1,000,000 g/mol)
  • Thermally stable up to 100°C
  • pH range stability from 3.5 to 11
  • Swelling capacity up to 40 times its weight
  • Excellent film-forming and adhesive characteristics
