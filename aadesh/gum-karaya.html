About Gum Karaya

Gum Karaya, obtained from Sterculia urens, is a high-molecular-weight acidic polysaccharide known for outstanding swelling, adhesion and thermal stability. Our product is sustainably harvested and processed to meet food and pharma requirements.

Functional Highlights

  • Thermally stable up to ~100°C
  • pH stability across 3.5–11
  • Synergizes with xanthan and guar to boost viscosity
  • Forms flexible, water-resistant films — excellent for adhesives & coatings

FAQ

Q: How is Gum Karaya different from Gum Arabic?

A: Karaya is adhesive, film-forming and swelling (partial solubility). Gum Arabic is fully water-soluble and excels in microencapsulation and beverage emulsions.

Q: Packaging & shelf life?

A: Available in 25kg multi-wall bags with moisture barrier or 50kg HDPE bags. Shelf life typically 18–24 months when stored dry.
