Product Overview

Sodium Hydrosulphite is a strong, water-soluble reducing agent widely used in textile processing (vat dyeing & discharge printing), pulp & paper bleaching, kaolin whitening, and industrial cleaning. It reduces dyes to their soluble leuco forms, strips color selectively, and brightens mineral clays. Primary Applications

Textiles: Vat dyeing (indigo), color stripping, discharge printing and brightening. Converts insoluble dyes to soluble leuco forms for penetration and fixation.

Pulp & Paper: Brightening recycled pulp and de-inking applications.

Kaolin & Minerals: Clay bleaching to remove iron stains and improve whiteness for ceramics, paper and paints.

Industrial Cleaning: Rust stain removers and specialty cleaning agents for metal surfaces and equipment.

Leather & Photography: Used in leather processing and some photographic solutions.

Storage & Safety

Sodium Hydrosulphite is moisture-sensitive and decomposes on contact with water or acids. Store in tightly sealed moisture-proof containers in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area away from oxidizers and acids. Avoid breathing dust — use appropriate PPE (gloves, goggles, respirator if dusty). Consult SDS for first-aid and disposal procedures. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
