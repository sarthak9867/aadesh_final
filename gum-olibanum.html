Product Overview

Gum Olibanum (Frankincense) is an oleo-gum resin primarily from Boswellia serrata. It typically contains resin (60–70%), gum (20–30%) and essential oil (5–10%). Dharma Hydrocolloid supplies high-purity tears and granules — graded and tested for boswellic acid content and aromatic quality.

Key Specifications

  • Appearance: Pale yellow to amber tears / granules
  • Resin content: ≥65% (dry basis)
  • Boswellic acids: ≥20%
  • Essential oil: 5–8%
  • Moisture: ≤6%
  • Solubility: soluble in alcohol; dispersible in water

Applications & Benefits

Aromatherapy & Incense: premium incense tears, essential oils for relaxation and meditation blends.

Therapeutic & Pharmaceutical: anti-inflammatory (boswellic acids), pain management formulations, topical and respiratory uses.

Food & Beverage: traditional flavouring, chewing gum base, functional food ingredients when processed to food-grade standards.

Cosmetics & Personal Care: anti-aging serums, balms and formulations for skin regeneration and scalp health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What species produce Gum Olibanum?

A: Primarily Boswellia serrata in India; other species include B. sacra and related Boswellia species.

Q2: How is boswellic acid content tested?

A: HPLC analysis is used to determine boswellic acid profile and concentration.
